AUGMENTATION METHODOLOGY
------------------------
Augmentations are applied ONLY to the test set during 5-fold cross-validation.
The model is trained on clean data and evaluated on perturbed data.
This simulates real-world deployment where training data is curated but
inference encounters adverse environmental conditions.

Augmentation Functions:
- FOG:    Reduces contrast by blending with mean, adds Gaussian noise
          Simulates hazy/misty conditions common in early morning UAV flights
          
- GLARE:  Increases brightness on random 30% of samples
          Simulates sun reflection on leaves/bolls during midday flights
          
- SHADOW: Reduces intensity on random 40% of samples
          Simulates cloud shadows or tree canopy shadows on field edges

Intensity Parameter: 0.3 (30% perturbation strength)

================================================================================
CONCLUSIONS
================================================================================

1. FEATURE SELECTION IS ESSENTIAL
   Using all 14 features leads to overfitting and catastrophic failure
   under environmental perturbations (45% accuracy under fog).

2. MI-4 SHOWS BEST OVERALL ROBUSTNESS
   Classical Mutual Information selection achieves 97% average accuracy
   across all conditions. However, it has higher feature redundancy.

3. QFS-4 PROVIDES BALANCED PERFORMANCE
   Quantum-selected features maintain ~89% average accuracy with
   lower redundancy (|r|=0.596 vs 0.798 for MI-4).

4. FEWER FEATURES CAN BE BETTER
   QFS-2 (only 2 features) achieves 94.6% average robustness,
   demonstrating that minimal, well-chosen features outperform
   large feature sets.

5. FOG IS THE HARDEST CONDITION
   All methods show largest degradation under fog augmentation.
   This suggests fog-specific training data augmentation may be
   needed for robust deployment.
